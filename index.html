Industries

One principle, every regulated industry.

Wherever valuable data is locked in place by regulation, confidentiality, or competition — the work can go to the data instead.

Healthcare & life science

Hospitals and labs collaborate on patient and genomic data across borders — AI models train and analyze without a single record leaving the clinic.

Government & public sector

Agencies compute on each other's registries — within and between authorities — while each remains the sole custodian of its citizens' data.

Finance & insurance

Banks and insurers run joint fraud and risk models on combined signals — with every institution's transactions staying in-house.

Intra-corporate

Business units and subsidiaries share AI workloads across legal entities and jurisdictions without moving data across the lines that separate them.

Research & academia

Universities and institutes pool rare datasets for joint studies — each partner keeps full control, every access is attributed and traceable.

Data economies

Data owners in any industry sell access instead of copies — monetizing datasets as a recurring asset while retaining ownership and auditability.
